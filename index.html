Senior Research Associate · University of Toronto

Turning multi-omic and clinical data into insight on cardiovascular disease.

I lead the mass spectrometry facility in the Department of Physiology and the Translational Biology and Engineering Program (TBEP), and manage multidisciplinary projects that bring together basic scientists, clinicians and industry partners. My work combines systems and computational biology with patient data to discover and validate biomarkers of heart failure.

Research and leadership

Multi-omics & systems biology

Integrating proteomic, phosphoproteomic and transcriptomic data with network biology to map disease mechanisms in human heart tissue and plasma.

Clinical data & prediction

Linking circulating biomarkers to patient cohorts and ICES population data to build machine-learning models of heart-failure outcomes.

Facility & project leadership

Running a core facility, coordinating academic, hospital and industry partners, and mentoring 15+ trainees and staff.
